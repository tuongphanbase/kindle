Nine small tic-tac-toe boards make one big board. Win a small board with three in a row to claim it; claim three small boards in a row to win. The catch: the square you play in decides which small board your opponent must play in next. Grey squares are boards you can't play in this turn. If you are sent to a board that is already won or full, you may play anywhere. Won boards are shown filled with the winner's letter; the last move is underlined.
