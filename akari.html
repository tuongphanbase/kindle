Light Up. Place lamps (black discs) on white squares so that every white square is lit. A lamp lights its whole row and column until a black wall stops it; lit squares turn grey. Two lamps may not shine on each other (they turn hollow if they do). A number on a wall says exactly how many lamps touch it (up, down, left, right). Tap: empty → lamp → × (your note: no lamp) → empty.
