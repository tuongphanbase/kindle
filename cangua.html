Ludo. Players A (black), B (white), C (grey) and D (double ring) race their pieces once round the board and up their own grey home column to the centre. Press Roll, then tap the piece you want to move (movable pieces are shaded). Roll a 1 or 6 to bring a piece out onto your start square (thick border). Rolling a 6 gives you another roll. Landing on an opponent sends it back to its yard, except on a start square. You need the exact number to reach the centre. First to bring every piece home wins.
