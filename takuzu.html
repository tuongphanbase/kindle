Binary puzzle. Fill every square with 0 or 1 (tap to change: blank → 0 → 1 → blank). Rules: no more than two of the same number next to each other in a row or column; every row and column has as many 0s as 1s; and no two rows (or two columns) are the same. Grey squares are given. Every puzzle can be solved by logic, without guessing.
