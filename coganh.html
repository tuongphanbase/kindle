How to play. Each side has 8 pieces. Take turns moving one piece one step along a line to an empty point. White moves first. Pieces are never removed — they change sides:

  • Gánh (carry): move into a point between two enemy pieces on one line, and both become yours.
  • Vây (surround): enemy pieces that can no longer move after your move become yours.
  • Mở (open): if your opponent moves away and leaves a point where you can gánh, you must gánh there.

Win by turning all of your opponent's pieces into yours. Your game is saved automatically.
