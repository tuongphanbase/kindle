How to play. One Hùm (tiger) starts in the middle, surrounded by 16 Trâu (buffaloes) on the edge. Trâu moves first. Every piece moves one step along a line to an empty point.

  • Hùm can also jump over one Trâu next to it, along a line, onto the empty point behind it, eating that Trâu. Hùm wins after eating 6 Trâu.
  • Trâu cannot jump. Trâu win by trapping Hùm so it has no move.

Your game is saved automatically.
