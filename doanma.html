Mastermind. The computer hides a secret code. Type a guess and press Guess. For each guess you are told how many digits are the right digit in the right place, and how many are a right digit but in the wrong place. Crack the code within 10 guesses. Easy: 4 different digits from 1–6. Medium: 4 digits from 1–6, repeats allowed. Hard: 5 digits from 1–8, repeats allowed.
