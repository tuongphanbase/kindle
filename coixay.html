Level:

Nine Men's Morris. White starts. First take turns placing your 9 pieces on empty points. Then take turns moving a piece along a line to a neighbouring empty point. Make a mill — three of your pieces in a straight line — and you remove one of your opponent's pieces (not one in a mill, unless all of them are). When you are down to 3 pieces you may jump to any empty point. You lose with fewer than 3 pieces or no move.
