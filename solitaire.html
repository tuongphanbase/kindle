Klondike Solitaire. Build the four piles at the top right from Ace to King in one suit. In the seven columns, stack cards downwards in alternating colours (red cards are grey with hollow suits). Tap a card to pick it (and the cards on top of it) up, then tap where it should go — or tap it again to move it somewhere automatically. Tap the deck at the top left to turn over cards. Only a King can go into an empty column.
