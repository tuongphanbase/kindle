Checkers (8×8 draughts). Black moves first. Pieces move one square diagonally forward onto a dark square. Capture by jumping over an enemy piece onto the empty square behind it. Capturing is compulsory, and if you can keep jumping you must — tap the final square and the whole jump is made. A piece that reaches the far side becomes a king (K) and can move and capture backwards too. You lose when you have no pieces or no moves left.
