Southern Vietnamese Tiến Lên against three computer players. Cards rank 3 (lowest) … K, A, 2 (highest); suits rank ♠ < ♣ < ♢ < ♡. Tap cards to select them, then press Đánh. You can play a single, a pair, three or four of a kind, a run of 3+ in a row (no 2s), or 3+ pairs in a row (đôi thông). To follow, play the same kind and size but higher, or press Bỏ — once you pass you are out until the next round. Chopping: four of a kind or 3 đôi thông beat a single 2; four of a kind or 4 đôi thông beat a pair of 2s. The holder of 3♠ starts the first game; first to play every card wins.
