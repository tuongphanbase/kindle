Board:

Go. Black plays first. Take turns placing a stone on an empty point. A group of stones with no empty point next to it is captured. You may not play a move that leaves your own stones with no liberties (unless it captures), or that repeats the position from just before your opponent's move (ko). When both players pass in a row the game ends and is scored: your stones plus the empty areas only you surround, and White gets 6.5 points (komi). Capture any dead stones before passing. The computer is a beginner-level player.
