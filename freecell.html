FreeCell. Every card is face up. Build the four home piles (top right) up from Ace to King in one suit. In the eight columns, stack cards downwards in alternating colours (red cards are grey with hollow suits). The four free cells (top left) each hold one card. Tap a card to pick it up (with the cards below it, if they are in order), then tap where it goes — or tap it again to send it somewhere automatically. Almost every deal can be won.
