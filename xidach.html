Nhà cái (Dealer)
You

Vietnamese Blackjack. Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. An Ace counts 10 or 11 in a two-card hand, 1 or 10 with three cards, and 1 with four or more. Special hands: Xì bàn (two Aces) beats everything, then Xì dách (Ace + 10, J, Q or K), then Ngũ linh (five cards, not over 21). You need at least 16 to stand. Over 21 is quắc (bust). Special hands pay double. If you run out of chips you get 100 more.
